# AeroResilience 🌿💙

### AI for Clean Air & Climate Resilience

AeroResilience is an AI-powered environmental intelligence platform designed to help communities understand air pollution and climate-related risks through data-driven insights and spatial intelligence.

The project combines curated environmental datasets, spatial AI engines, and analytical workflows to identify pollution patterns, assess vulnerable areas, and support informed climate resilience planning.

## Key Focus Areas

* 🌿 Air pollution analysis and environmental monitoring
* 🌍 Climate resilience and vulnerability assessment
* 🗺️ Spatial intelligence and risk analysis
* 🤖 AI-assisted environmental insights
* 📊 Data-driven decision support

## Project Goal

Our goal is to transform complex environmental data into actionable insights that can support cleaner air, healthier communities, stronger climate resilience, and a more sustainable future.
